Engineered for Modern Antigravity

Everything you need for seamless dual-application workspace isolation.

⚡

Dual-App Orchestration

Google recently split Antigravity into two apps: the IDE and the Companion Chat. Multigravity Duo supports, clones, and isolates both simultaneously.

🔒

Zero Account Collision

Every profile possesses its own isolated user-data store, credential storage, and cookies. Log in with different Google accounts concurrently without conflicts.

🍃

Auth-Only Mode

Share your installed extensions and settings via symlinks while keeping Google accounts strictly isolated. Drops disk consumption from 500MB to just 2MB per profile.

🖥️

Native Launcher Apps

Automatically creates clickable `.app` bundles on macOS, `.desktop` launchers on Linux, and Start Menu shortcuts on Windows for instant access.

🎯

Flexible Launch Flags

Launch Antigravity IDE by default, or use --chat for the companion window, or --all to spin up the complete duo in one keystroke.

📦

Portable Blueprint Templates

Export configured profiles as reusable templates or tar.gz archives. Onboard new teammates or duplicate setups in seconds.
